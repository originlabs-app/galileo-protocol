W3C, 15 May 2025, section 1.1
Status information is about the verifiable credential The status information associated with a particular verifiable credential is about the verifiable credential itself and might not apply to any
underlying or backing credential , such as an educational degree. For
example, in the case of such an educational degree, it is possible for a verifiable credential to be revoked because the mechanism used to
create its digital signature has been compromised, while the backing educational
degree remains valid.
