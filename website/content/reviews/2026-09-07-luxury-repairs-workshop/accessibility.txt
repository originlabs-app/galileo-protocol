      uid=3_64 heading "Build the record as the job moves through the workshop" level="2"
      uid=3_65 StaticText "Do not leave the whole form until collection. Each stage should leave the next person a clear decision and the evidence behind it. The following flow is a proposed working method, not a brand's published process."
      uid=3_66 image "From intake to a repair record ready to share Identify the item and its condition, save the approved scope, record work and parts, then issue return checks and warranty terms.       uid=3_67 heading "At intake, anchor the record to the object" level="3"
      uid=3_68 StaticText "Give the job a reference distinct from the product identifier. Record the visible identifiers, model description, accessories received and photographs of the relevant areas. If an identifier cannot be read, say so and explain how the workshop matched the item to the job."
      uid=3_69 StaticText "Separate the customer's report from the workshop's observation. Customer reports clasp opens during use is different from workshop reproduced clasp opening under its stated check. For a watch, name the test and its result rather than writing works perfectly. For a bag, mark existing wear before authorising changes."
      uid=3_70 StaticText "If a tag or marked component is affected, preserve the old reference and explain the replacement link. Do not silently assign a new identity to the whole object. Our "
      uid=3_71 link "identifier analysis" url="http://127.0.0.1:3217/blog/2026-09-02-chanel-serial-code-not-identity"
        uid=3_72 StaticText "identifier analysis"
      uid=3_73 StaticText " covers why a number alone cannot establish authenticity."
      uid=3_74 heading "Before work, preserve what the customer approved" level="3"
      uid=3_75 StaticText "Link the diagnosis to a dated estimate and the accepted scope. Record who approved it through the workshop's normal customer channel. Keep the full approval in the private job file; the later buyer needs the scope, not the customer's email address."
      uid=3_76 StaticText "If the diagnosis changes, obtain the required approval for the revised work before proceeding under your agreed process. Keep declined treatments visible. A strap replacement does not authorise recolouring the bag, and an approved movement service should not silently become a cosmetic restoration."
      uid=3_77 heading "During repair, distinguish installed, removed and untouched parts" level="3"
      uid=3_78 StaticText "Describe each intervention and its date. For every replacement, identify the part and the supporting reference available from the workshop. Use maker-supplied only when there is evidence for that description. If origin is unconfirmed, record that limit instead of upgrading it to original."
      uid=3_79 StaticText "Record what happened to the removed part: returned, retained or disposed of under the agreed terms. Do not promise its return if the service terms provide otherwise. Note untouched components where a reader might otherwise infer a complete overhaul."
      uid=3_80 StaticText "Keep the completion note separate from the estimate. An estimate shows intended work; the completion note states what the workshop actually did. If they differ, explain the change and point to its approval."
      uid=3_81 heading "At collection, explain the result and its boundaries" level="3"
      uid=3_82 StaticText "Record the final checks, their author and the date. Describe the condition at return, including remaining marks, declined work and any issue awaiting follow-up. Let the customer compare the object with the written scope before acknowledging receipt."
      uid=3_83 StaticText "Copy or attach the actual service warranty terms with their provider and market. Include what is covered, the applicable dates, exclusions and the route for a claim. Check any conditions for a later owner rather than assuming coverage transfers."
      uid=3_84 StaticText "If the terms are missing, mark warranty documentation pending and ask the provider for it. Do not turn missing paperwork into a promise of coverage or a claim that no rights exist. This form does not determine the customer's legal rights."

      uid=3_127 heading "At resale, assess the item again" level="2"
      uid=3_128 StaticText "Treat the workshop record as evidence from its stated author. Confirm unclear points through a known workshop contact, especially where the document and physical condition disagree. An invoice alone may not settle part origin or the exact work completed."
      uid=3_129 StaticText "Keep three conclusions separate in the listing: the documented intervention, the reseller's present condition assessment and any independently supported authenticity finding.       uid=3_135 heading "Frequently asked questions" level="2"
      uid=3_136 heading "What should accompany a luxury item after repair?" level="3"

      uid=3_138 heading "Does a repair record prove authenticity or resale value?" level="3"
      uid=3_139 StaticText "No. It records what a stated workshop says it did to an identified item. Authenticity, ownership, present condition and resale valuation require their own evidence. A replacement part should be described without implying that the whole item has been authenticated."
      uid=3_140 heading "Does a repair restart the warranty on the whole product?" level="3"
      uid=3_141 StaticText "Do not assume that it does. Check the provider, country, service and written terms for that job. Record covered work or components, dates, exclusions and the claims route. If terms are missing, request them instead of inventing coverage or a duration."
